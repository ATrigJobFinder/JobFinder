# Version 19: cover letter craft, three-page Tailor, builder fixes

## Home
- New step 2, **Build a strong cover letter** (status from your saved letter: paragraphs, words, gaps). The plan is now seven steps.

## Cover letters (from the cover letter specification, `14a-cover-craft.js`)
- Craft rules go into every AI prompt: truth over polish, one specific example, about them proven by you, the swap test, plain voice, banned phrases.
- Checks run in code (never left to the AI): clichés and AI tells, dashes, exclamation marks, too many "I" openings, long sentences, repeated words, age/pay, figures not in your details. Shown on both cover letter pages.
- Each paragraph has "Why this paragraph, and how to write it" (what good looks like, the rule for your career stage, an example), **Write with AI** and **Reword** (up to three versions, checked against your facts).
- Minimum words are 20 or more higher for every paragraph (totals +100).
- Create or edit cover letter starts with a design picker, line spacing and page margins (same choices as the resume; Word, PDF and preview follow).
- Blank company or "addressed to" shows as [XXX]. Every gap is yellow in the letter, behind the text boxes and in the Word file. Letters can be saved, copied and downloaded with gaps.
- Input boxes start larger.

## Tailor to a job
- Page 1: job ad first, then resume plus an optional cover letter (toggle: both, resume only, letter only). Place your missing skills sits under the skill tables; its button says **Save answers to profile**; then a **Next** button.
- **Details** under the score breakdown jumps to What this job needs. Selection criteria and nice-to-haves use the must-have layout. The employer picker is gone (the company guide is found from the ad).
- Page 2: tailored resume, with the match score, every key skill highlighted and a chip list to jump to each.
- Page 3: criteria and cover letter, with design/spacing/margins, key skills highlighted, craft check and Save to my profile.

## Create or edit resume
- Start from my resume or LinkedIn is blue. Work-type grid folds after a pick. "Is this resume for one particular job?" removed. Template preview uses an example until you have details.
- Bullet points from your answers go straight in (with Undo). Polish removed from jobs and extras. Reword with up to three versions on projects and volunteering.
- Grades only appear when above GPA 3.5/4, 5.5/7, WAM 80% or ATAR 90. Double degrees. Suggest more on every skills box. Suggestion chips no longer open the list; picking from the list keeps it open.
- Choose what goes on this resume: vertical list with line counts, per-line removal, up/down order, and **Fit to one page** with a reason for everything it hides. Two other designs and a "new design?" box sit before Download.
- Spelling language (Australian, British, New Zealand, Canadian, American) drives autocorrect.
- The AI key box is no longer a password field, so browsers stop offering to save it.

Tested in headless Chrome: every view, the builder steps, the letter page, and the three Tailor pages, with no errors and no sideways scrolling at 400 px.
