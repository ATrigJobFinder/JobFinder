# Careers pages to check by hand

These employers are not in the Job Finder directory yet, because their websites would not let the automated check in or the check could not find a careers page. I did not try to get around the blocks. Open each website in your own browser, find the careers or jobs page, and tick it off. Send me the links you find and I will add them with a full profile.

How to read the notes:

- **Blocked**: the website refuses automated visitors. It usually opens fine in a normal browser.
- **No reply**: the automated check got no answer. The site may be down, slow, or blocking. Some of the addresses are my best guess, so if the site does not open, search for the employer by name.
- **No page found**: the website loads, but the check could not find a careers page. Look for Careers, Work with us, Jobs or Join us in the menu or footer.

Only use a link if it is on the employer's own website (not a job board such as Seek or Indeed). Brisbane Broncos is in the Sport list: its website tells automated tools not to read it, so it was left alone on purpose.
