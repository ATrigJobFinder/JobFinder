          How they're ranked, and how Job Finder uses this

          Links: every company link goes to the employer's own website, usually its careers page and, where it has one, its graduate or early-careers page. Tips taken from graduate review sites are named but not linked.

          Ranking: companies with a full guide are ranked by the 2026 SEEK Grad Top100, the student vote run by GradConnection (now SEEK Grad), from #1 EY down. Guides that weren't in it follow, biggest first, then every other employer, biggest first.

          Sizes: small is under 500 staff, medium 500 to 5,000, large over 5,000, and global means a large firm working in many countries. States: “National” means offices or roles across Australia.

          Sending your resume to many employers: tick each company, then Add to Application tracker. Each one is saved with its careers link, so you can work through them, tailor your resume for each, and record when you applied.

          In your applications: scan an ad from one of these companies in Tailor to a job and its values and tips appear in “What this job needs”, and go into your cover letter. “Our tip” marks our suggestion, based on what the company says it wants.

          Careers links checked October 2026 on each employer's own website. Guide tips come from company careers pages, plus graduate reviews where named. Programs, dates and rules change, so open the careers page before you apply.

            Add a job you found

            Paste the ad from anywhere. It's saved to the Application tracker, and if the same job turns up again it's merged, not repeated.

            How listings get here

            • Posted on Job Finder: employers post directly from Hiring.
            • Company career sites: a collector reads the public job feeds that companies' hiring systems publish (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, SmartRecruiters, Workable and others) and job pages marked up for search engines.
            • Job boards and aggregators: through their official APIs, such as Adzuna.
            • No duplicates: listings are matched on company, title, place, ad reference, link and wording. The company's own listing is kept, and the others are shown as “also listed on”.
