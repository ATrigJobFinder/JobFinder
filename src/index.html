                Company guides

                Australian employers, from small firms to global ones. Every link goes to the employer's own careers page. Companies with a full guide also have an About page, advice for applying, how to apply, and the jobs they have listed here. Tick the ones you want and add them to your Application tracker in one go.

                How they're ranked, and how Job Finder uses this

                Links: every company link goes to the employer's own website, usually its careers page and, where it has one, its graduate or early-careers page. Tips taken from graduate review sites are named but not linked.

                Ranking: companies with a full guide are ranked by the SEEK Grad Top100, the student vote run by GradConnection (now SEEK Grad), from #1 down. Guides that weren't in it follow, biggest first, then every other employer, biggest first.

                Sizes: small is under 500 staff, medium 500 to 5,000, large over 5,000, and global means a large firm working in many countries. States: “National” means offices or roles across Australia.

                Sending your resume to many employers: tick each company, then Add to Application tracker. Each one is saved with its careers link, so you can work through them, tailor your resume for each, and record when you applied.

                In your applications: scan an ad from one of these companies in Tailor to a job and its values and tips appear in “What this job needs”, and go into your cover letter. “Our tip” marks our suggestion, based on what the company says it wants.

                Careers links were checked on each employer's own website. Guide tips come from company careers pages, plus graduate reviews where named. Programs, dates and rules change, so open the careers page before you apply.

                Quick answers

                Where is my information saved?

                In this browser, unless you sign up. With an account, your resume, letters, applications and practice are saved to it and follow you to any device. Your AI key never leaves your browser.

                The AI is slow or gave a strange answer

                Open Choose your AI. A model in your browser or Chrome's built-in AI can take a minute on some computers; a free Gemini key answers in a few seconds. Always check AI wording before you use it.

                I changed something by mistake

                Press Ctrl+Z (Cmd+Z on a Mac), or the undo arrow at the top of the page.

                How do I delete my data?

                Create or edit profile, then "Delete everything". You can download a copy first.
